ABOUT OUR STORY

EDUCATION WITH A PURPOSE.

Founded in 2005 with a vision to deliver purposeful, value-driven education to rural youth, Shobha Education Trust has built an enduring institutional ecosystem in Jaunpur, Uttar Pradesh.

What began as a grassroots effort to democratize higher learning has expanded to encompass university undergraduate degrees, teacher training, industrial technical trades, and community healthcare outreach.

ENVIRONMENT CAMPUS & FACILITIES

Set within a serene 15-acre green environment in Kamruddinpur, Jaunpur, our campus is designed to support academic rigor, physical wellness, and practical skill-building.

15-Acre Green Campus

Spacious, distraction-free environment conducive to disciplined study, reflection, and community learning.

Modern Computer Lab

Digital workstation facility offering high-speed connectivity and foundational IT training for rural students.

Sports & Athletics Grounds

Outdoor playing fields for volleyball, cricket, running tracks, and annual student sports competitions.

Dedicated Yoga & Wellness

Regular guided physical conditioning, yoga sessions, and mental well-being practices on campus.

Purified RO Water

Clean and chilled drinking water filtration systems installed across all institutional blocks.

Regional Bus Transport

Institutional bus service connecting rural towns, villages, and transport hubs to the campus.
