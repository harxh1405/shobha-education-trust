ESTABLISHED WITH A PURPOSE

Shobha Education Trust was established in 2005 in Jaunpur, Uttar Pradesh, as a non-profit charitable educational organization dedicated to educational empowerment, character building, and vocational skill development.

Recognizing that quality higher education and technical training were predominantly concentrated in urban centers, the Trust undertook a conscious mission to bridge the rural-urban divide, bringing accredited academic disciplines directly to the youth of eastern Uttar Pradesh.

Today, the Trust serves as the governing institutional umbrella for Sarveshwaree Mahavidyalaya, Sarveshwaree Private ITI, our emerging Sarveshwaree Paraveterinary initiative, and ongoing community-centered welfare programs.

OUR STORY
SARVESHWAREE MAHAVIDYALAYA CAMPUS JAUNPUR, UP

FROM A RURAL VISION
TO AN EDUCATIONAL JOURNEY.

The institution was established with the intention of bringing meaningful educational opportunities closer to communities in a remote, predominantly rural part of Jaunpur.

The founding vision was not simply to create another college, but to build an accessible, supportive educational environment where learners could pursue university undergraduate degrees, technical vocational trades, and future career pathways without being forced to migrate far from their families and communities.

Under the inspirational guidance of Parampujya Baba Gurupad Sambhav Ram Ji, the spiritual successor and current head associated with Sri Sarveshwari Samooh, Padav, Varanasi, the Trust was established on values of selfless service, moral integrity, and social upliftment.

“Education is the most potent catalyst to guide humanity from ignorance to enlightenment — अँधेरे से उजाले की ओर. Our responsibility is to ensure that light shines in every village.”
PARMPUJYA BABA GURUPAD SAMBHAV RAM JI

OUR GUIDING INSPIRATION

PARMPUJYA BABA
GURUPAD SAMBHAV RAM JI

“Knowledge that does not alleviate the suffering of society and awaken compassion in the heart remains incomplete. Real education must serve the neglected and empower the rural soul.”

As the spiritual successor and current head associated with Sri Sarveshwari Samooh, Padav, Varanasi, Parmpujya Baba Gurupad Sambhav Ram Ji has consistently championed social welfare, healthcare for the underprivileged, and educational accessibility in rural India.

His moral vision and compassionate ideals inspired the foundation of Shobha Education Trust in 2005. Rather than focusing on conventional commercialization, the Trust was mandated to dedicate its energies to rural youth, female learners, and vocational empowerment.

His presence remains our institutional compass — reminding faculty, administrators, and students alike that learning attains its highest dignity when joined with humility, ethical discipline, and active community service.

FROM THE DESK OF THE MANAGER

BUILDING ROOTED PATHWAYS FOR RURAL YOUTH

“When we laid the cornerstone of Shobha Education Trust in 2005, our purpose was clear: no talented village youth should be denied higher learning or vocational dignity simply because of where they were born.”

For generations across eastern Uttar Pradesh, rural families faced an agonizing dilemma: either send their children far away to expensive metropolitan centers, or watch their educational aspirations wither. In Kamruddinpur, Rampur, Jaunpur, we resolved to change that reality permanently.

Guided by the ethical ideals of Parmpujya Baba Gurupad Sambhav Ram Ji, we established Sarveshwaree Mahavidyalaya in 2010 to provide university undergraduate studies affiliated with Veer Bahadur Singh Purvanchal University. In 2016, recognizing that strong primary schools need disciplined, qualified local teachers, we introduced the D.El.Ed (BTC) pathway. In 2018, we built Sarveshwaree Private ITI with modern workshops to equip rural youth with certified industrial trades in Electrician and Fitter.

Throughout this journey, our founding philosophy remains uncompromised: zero capitation, zero hidden fees, and absolute transparency. As we now prepare emerging initiatives in paraveterinary and agricultural education, our promise to every family remains unchanged — disciplined education, authentic mentorship, and honest service.

Dr. Parmendra Kumar Singh Founder & Manager, Shobha Education Trust

FROM THE DESK OF THE DIRECTOR

ACADEMIC RIGOR, VOCATIONAL EXCELLENCE & MORAL DISCIPLINE

“A college degree is only as valuable as the competence, moral spine, and genuine self-reliance it instills in the student. Our classroom is a crucible for real capability.”

As educators, our duty goes far beyond completing syllabi and holding examinations. In a developing regional economy, students need both conceptual depth in humanities and hard practical skills in technical fields to compete with confidence.

Across Sarveshwaree Mahavidyalaya and Sarveshwaree Private ITI, we insist on continuous lecture attendance, direct laboratory practice, and rigorous workshop discipline. Our faculty members work closely with students from rural backgrounds, cultivating analytical thinking, spoken communication, and digital fluency.

Equal opportunity is at the core of our daily operations. Through initiatives like the Saheli Book Bank, we ensure that no female student or economically challenged learner is held back by the inability to purchase prescribed textbooks. Coupled with physical athletics, yoga conditioning, and community service camps, we prepare graduates who are academically capable, technically proficient, and morally grounded.

Dr. Sanjay Kumar Singh Director, Shobha Education Trust

OUR PURPOSE

VISION

“Meaningful education and skill development should be accessible to learners and communities, enabling individuals to become responsible, capable and confident contributors to society.”

We envision an educational landscape where geography and socioeconomic background never limit a student's aspirations, fostering self-reliance and civic responsibility across eastern Uttar Pradesh.

MISSION

▪ Accessible Higher Education: Provide quality university undergraduate education right at the doorstep of rural communities.
▪ Practical & Vocational Pathways: Deliver industry-recognized technical trade skills through modern ITI workshops.
▪ Real-World Skill Integration: Bridge textbook concepts with hands-on application, problem-solving, and digital tools.
▪ Socioeconomic Support: Ensure barrier-free education for female students and economically challenged families through initiatives like the Saheli Book Bank.
▪ Community Service Ethos: Cultivate social responsibility through regular medical camps, environmental awareness, and rural health drives.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Our four fundamental institutional pillars guide every academic decision, infrastructural expansion, and student initiative across Shobha Education Trust.

01

ACCESS

Education should reach communities that need it. High quality academic and vocational infrastructure must be situated where rural students can access it without economic or geographic exclusion.

02

OPPORTUNITY

Learning should create reliable pathways toward employment, self-reliance, and personal confidence. We empower every learner to realize their potential in a rapidly evolving economy.

03

PRACTICAL LEARNING

Education must connect classroom knowledge with real-world skills. Through laboratory experiments, ITI workshops, and practical problem solving, students gain tangible capabilities.

04

SERVICE

An educational institution should be an active contributor to the community around it. We instill the value of service through community health camps, social initiatives, and civic responsibility.
