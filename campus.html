SETTING THE 15-ACRE ENVIRONMENT

SPACE TO LEARN,
THINK & GROW

Spread across 15 acres in Kamruddinpur, Rampur, Jaunpur, the institutional grounds offer an expansive campus sheltered from urban distractions.

The layout integrates multi-story academic wings for Sarveshwaree Mahavidyalaya, dedicated heavy-duty workshops for Sarveshwaree Private ITI, computing facilities, expansive playing grounds, and community gathering areas.

FACILITIES STUDENT AMENITIES

Carefully planned infrastructure ensures that every learner has access to modern educational tools, hygiene, physical wellness, and reliable regional connectivity.

Modern Computer Laboratory

Air-cooled computer lab equipped with high-speed broadband internet and individual workstations for digital literacy, curriculum coursework, and online research.

Technical ITI Workshops

Specialized practical training bays equipped with lathe machines, electrical panels, testing equipment, motor rewinding setups, and standard industrial safety apparatus.

Institutional Library & Reading Room

Comprehensive repository of university textbooks, reference volumes, competitive examination manuals, and daily periodicals supporting quiet study.

Sports & Athletics Grounds

Multi-purpose outdoor fields supporting volleyball, cricket, athletic sprints, and team sports, hosting annual inter-institutional sports competitions.

Yoga & Physical Conditioning

Dedicated open spaces and guidance for daily yoga practice, pranayama, and physical discipline fostering mental clarity and holistic wellness.

Purified Chilled RO Drinking Water

Commercial-grade Reverse Osmosis (RO) water purification units with chilled dispensers installed across classroom wings and workshops.

Institutional Bus Transportation

Dedicated bus and transit network operating on key regional routes connecting surrounding rural communities, towns, and transit junctions to the campus.

Power Backup & Campus Security

Dedicated electrical generator backup ensuring uninterrupted laboratory and workshop sessions, supported by round-the-clock campus security personnel.
