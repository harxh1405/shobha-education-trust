EDUCATION ROOTED IN
COMMUNITY & SERVICE.

Learning does not end at the classroom door. Through healthcare camps, book banks, and rural outreach, Shobha Education Trust actively serves the wider community of Jaunpur.

FREE EYE CARE & HEALTH CONSULTATION CAMP Hosted regularly at the Sarveshwaree campus for rural villagers and families.
HEALTHCARE COMMUNITY EYE-CARE CAMPS

FREE HEALTH SERVICES
FOR OUR REGION

Recognizing the lack of specialized ophthalmic healthcare in nearby villages, the Trust regularly partners with medical professionals to organize free eye-screening diagnostic camps on campus.

Hundreds of rural elders and children receive free eye examinations, diagnostic screenings for cataract detection, and guidance on preventive vision health without any fee burden.

OUTREACH KEY SOCIAL INITIATIVES
Saheli Book Bank

A community-supported textbook repository that loans complete curriculum textbooks free of cost to women and underprivileged students for their entire degree duration.

Free Medical & Health Camps

Periodic general health checkup camps, vision diagnostics, and maternal healthcare awareness workshops conducted on our 15-acre campus grounds.

Yoga & Moral Culture

Guided daily yoga sessions and value-based moral education inspired by the teachings of Parmpujya Baba Gurupad Sambhav Ram Ji, emphasizing humility and truth.

Rural Literacy Support

Student volunteers and faculty engage with surrounding rural hamlets to counsel parents on the importance of uninterrupted education for daughters.
