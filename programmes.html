01 / HIGHER EDUCATION

B.A. (BACHELOR OF ARTS)

  • Institution: Sarveshwaree Mahavidyalaya
  • Affiliation: Veer Bahadur Singh Purvanchal University, Jaunpur
  • College Code: 0694
  • Minimum Eligibility: 10+2 (Intermediate) in any stream from a recognized board
  • Programme Duration: 3-Year Undergraduate Degree

The Bachelor of Arts programme provides students with comprehensive critical thinking, analytical abilities, and regional cultural knowledge. Students can select tailored subject combinations designed to prepare them for public civil service examinations, higher postgraduate education, and teaching careers.

02 / TEACHER EDUCATION

D.El.Ed / BTC

Diploma in Elementary Education (formerly BTC) is a foundational 2-year professional training programme preparing future teachers for primary and upper-primary schooling across Uttar Pradesh.

  • Programme Introduction: 2016
  • Approved Intake: 50 Seats per batch
  • Minimum Eligibility: Graduation from a recognized university
  • Statutory Recognition: National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), New Delhi
  • Examining Authority: Pariksha Niyamak Pradhikari (PNP), Prayagraj
  • Admission Mode: Centralized state rank-based online counseling by UP Government
Official Annual Training Fee
₹41,000 per annum (Two years course)
