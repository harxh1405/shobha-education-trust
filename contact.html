HOW TO REACH THE CAMPUS

Sarveshwaree Mahavidyalaya and Sarveshwaree Private ITI are situated in Kamruddinpur, Noorpur, Rampur, Jaunpur. The campus is well-connected through the regional highway network connecting Jaunpur, Bhadohi, and Varanasi. Frequent road transport buses and regional passenger services run directly to Rampur town.
