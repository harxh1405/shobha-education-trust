01 / HIGHER EDUCATION

SARVESHWAREE
MAHAVIDYALAYA

Established in 2010, Sarveshwaree Mahavidyalaya serves as the flagship undergraduate higher educational institution under Shobha Education Trust. Situated on a serene 15-acre green campus in Kamruddinpur, Rampur, Jaunpur, the college provides accessible, university-affiliated degree education to thousands of rural learners.

  • Establishment Year: 2010
  • University Affiliation: Veer Bahadur Singh Purvanchal University, Jaunpur
  • College Code: 0694
  • Location: Kamruddinpur, Post Rampur, District Jaunpur, Uttar Pradesh – 222203
  • Initial Academic Offering: Bachelor of Arts (B.A.)

The institution is dedicated to academic rigor, moral character, and intellectual freedom. It offers undergraduate studies across key disciplines in humanities, languages, and social sciences, supported by dedicated departmental faculty, a rich institutional library, and expansive sports grounds.

D.El.Ed / BTC

Statutory Recognition & Affiliation

Recognized by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), New Delhi, and examined by Pariksha Niyamak Pradhikari (PNP), Prayagraj.

02

Minimum Eligibility: Graduation from a recognized university.

Counseling & Admission Route

Admission is conducted through the centralized, state rank-based online counseling process as conducted by the Uttar Pradesh state government authority.

02 / TECHNICAL & VOCATIONAL EDUCATION

SARVESHWAREE
PRIVATE ITI

Established in 2018, Sarveshwaree Private ITI addresses the critical need for hands-on, job-ready technical trade skills among rural youth. The institute features dedicated practical machine shops, wiring labs, precision measurement instruments, and certified trainers.

  • Established: 2018
  • NCVT Code: PR09900997
  • SCVT Code: 3938
  • Eligibility: High School (10th) or Intermediate (10+2)
VIEW ITI PROGRAMMES  →
TRADE: ELECTRICIAN 120 SEATS

Comprehensive practical training in electrical installations, industrial motor winding, domestic wiring, transformers, safety protocols, and commercial power maintenance.

TRADE: FITTER 120 SEATS

Precision lathe work, metal assembly, fabrication, bench fitting, blueprint reading, and mechanical maintenance preparing students for manufacturing industries.

03 / EMERGING PROFESSIONAL PATHWAYS
UNDER PROCESS / COMING SOON

SARVESHWAREE
PARAVETERINARY INITIATIVE

Sarveshwaree Paraveterinary Diploma Pariksha Sansthan represents Shobha Education Trust's visionary initiative to serve agricultural livelihoods through animal healthcare education.

In agrarian communities across Uttar Pradesh, livestock forms the financial lifeline of families. This initiative aims to prepare certified veterinary assistants, livestock supervisors, and pharmacy personnel equipped with scientific knowledge.

STATUS NOTICE UNDER PROCESS

This programme is currently under statutory preparation and procedural approval. No admissions are open at this time.
