INSTITUTIONAL QR PAYMENT WITH MANUAL DESK VERIFICATION

Candidates reporting to the campus admissions cell at Kamruddinpur, Jaunpur can complete fee payment using our official UPI QR code at the counter. The payment is manually reconciled and authorized by staff on the premises:

  • 1. Scan & Pay: Scan the counter QR code above with any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) for the fixed course fee.
  • 2. Provide Details: Submit candidate's Name, Home District, Phone Number, and 12-digit UTR/Reference ID at the desk.
  • 3. Instant Reconciliation: The admissions officer validates the credit against institutional bank records.
  • 4. Authorized Receipt: An official electronic institutional receipt with verification hash is immediately generated.
